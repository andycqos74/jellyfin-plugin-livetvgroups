Jellyfin reads group-title from your M3U playlists and then discards it, so no client can browse Live TV by category. This plugin reads the playlists back, parses the groups, and serves them at GET /LiveTvGroups/Groups keyed to Jellyfin channel ids.

Nothing to configure to get started: the playlists come from the M3U tuners already set up under Dashboard → Live TV.

Categories

Order is what clients show. Move a category up or down to change its position, or hide it to leave it out entirely. Categories your playlist gains later are added to the end automatically, so nothing disappears while you are not looking.

Reading the playlist

Playlists change rarely and can be several megabytes. 0 re-reads on every request. Category order and visibility apply immediately either way.
Also collects channels that no playlist entry matched, so nothing is unreachable.
